## Source evidence and limited change
- `DX11VideoProcessor.cpp` creates windowed flip chains with `DXGI_SCALING_NONE`. A smaller old buffer cannot cover a larger client while pending. `PresentCompletedVideo` already maps into that old buffer; a shader alone cannot fill unavailable pixels. `SetWindowRect` coalesces changes for 140 ms after the latest event. B09 `NextCompletedFrame` only commits with an empty queue, failed clock, or > submit lead + 2 ms remaining before head. During due-head/slow-Present conditions this can defer forever; no independent maximum age for expansion. This is a plausible explanation, not measured proof of this screenshot.
- Only production change is `NextCompletedFrame` resize eligibility: for FLIP_DISCARD or FLIP_SEQUENTIAL with target width or height larger than committed window, after 140 + 50 ms since latest event allow existing commit even with due head. Earlier safe gap still wins. Shrink, same-size, legacy and non-scheduled path unchanged. Failed ResizeBuffers uses original rollback and resets last-change tick for retry. B11 source-turn handling remains ahead of resize; its absolute deadline expires, worker re-reads schedule before Present. No new waits. No change to B08/B09 producer admission or B11 fairness, Fence, PTS, VE or exit. This is only a maximum additional scheduling deferral at an eligible callback; OS lock, ongoing driver call, coalescing and retry can extend visible delay. 50 ms is a chosen conservative engineering default, not measured optimum.
